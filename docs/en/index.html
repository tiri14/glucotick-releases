The Windows app is currently in Spanish. These English guides keep the actual menu labels so you can follow each step.

For FreeStyle Libre users and authorized caregivers

GlucoTick shows readings already available in the official LibreLinkUp app. It can be used by the person wearing the sensor or an authorized relative or caregiver.

Sensor → compatible FreeStyle Libre mobile app → LibreLinkUp → GlucoTick on Windows. The sharing phone and PC need Internet. GlucoTick does not read the sensor directly, and the PC need not be near it. Compatibility depends on the sensor system, app, phone and region.

The panel also shows measurement time, connection status and a graph of the last three hours of readings received in the current session. Click the icon to open it. Closing the panel leaves the app running; use Salir in the tray menu to exit.

Start with your receiving account

  1. Check your sensor app. Your compatible FreeStyle Libre mobile app must receive readings. The sharing phone needs an Internet connection.
  2. Share with LibreLinkUp. Under sharing or connected apps, invite the email address of the receiving account. Menu labels vary by app and region.
  3. Accept the invitation. In the official LibreLinkUp app, verify the email address, accept the applicable terms and the invitation, and check that the person and reading appear.
  4. Install GlucoTick. Download the latest Setup and follow the installer. The “Source code” files are not installers.
  5. Connect the receiving account. In Ajustes → Cuenta, enter the LibreLinkUp credentials, choose Conectar y comprobar cuenta, select the person and save.
  6. Choose a view. Use Visualización to enable the tray or large number. Windows 11 x64 can also use the optional Windhawk clock wizard.

Requirements

Windows 10 version 2004 or later, or Windows 11; an x64 PC; .NET Framework 4.8; Internet; and a LibreLinkUp account that already receives shared readings. The integrated clock requires Windows 11 x64. Sensor, app and regional compatibility depend on the Abbott system; support for every sensor or other brands is not promised.

First clock setup may need up to 313 MiB of downloads, 4 GB free and administrator permission. The wizard checks its validated Windhawk combination and offers setup, repair or removal. Unvalidated versions are preserved without automatic downgrades.

Settings for your day

Choose light, dark or system theme; mg/dL or mmol/L; start with Windows; and placement of the large number. Color thresholds apply to all views; optional notification thresholds are configured separately. Private mode hides the person's name, readings and graph and pauses glucose notices while the connection continues.

Reading age stays visible

After more than five minutes from measurement, the last value turns grey in all views. Fetching the same reading does not reset its age. Check the phone and LibreLinkUp when readings stop arriving.

Updates when you choose

GlucoTick checks for new releases every two hours, with up to five minutes of variation, and on startup or when opening the panel or updates. You choose when to install. The updater verifies the package and keeps your account and preferences; save pending settings first.

Current installers have no trusted publisher signature. Download only from the official repository. A SHA-256 checksum verifies integrity but is not a publisher signature. Uninstalling through Windows lets you preserve or delete local account and settings; Windhawk is kept.

Privacy and support

Credentials are encrypted for your Windows user. Readings and the graph remain in session memory. The optional clock bridge uses a local file with reading, trend and time; it contains no account or person identifier and is removed when you exit or disable integration. GlucoTick connects to LibreLinkUp and GitHub, with no own telemetry or automatic diagnostic uploads. Read the privacy details.

Independent companion viewer

GlucoTick is not an official Abbott app. It does not calculate insulin doses or replace your primary FreeStyle Libre device, app, alarms or clinical advice. Shared readings may arrive late. Use your primary system for treatment decisions.
