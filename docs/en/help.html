No readings in GlucoTick

  1. Check that the main FreeStyle Libre mobile app receives a sensor reading.
  2. Check Internet access on both the sharing phone and PC.
  3. Open LibreLinkUp with the receiving account. The person and reading must appear there.
  4. If missing, check email verification, the accepted sharing invitation and applicable service terms in LibreLinkUp. Official guide.
  5. Once LibreLinkUp shows readings, connect that same account in GlucoTick, choose the person and save.

Step-by-step installation and setup.

From GlucoTick 0.8.10, a temporary empty connection response from LibreLinkUp triggers automatic retries, with waits of two to five minutes. Readings resume when the connection returns. While waiting, the last reading keeps its timestamp and turns grey after five minutes.

The account will not connect

Check sign-in in the official LibreLinkUp app first. Use the receiving account, not just the sensor-app credentials. Check for pending terms or invitations. If the service limits requests, wait before retrying; repeated requests do not remove the limit.

Compatibility may vary by region or change with the external service. Never post credentials, health readings or screenshots with personal information in public issues.

The reading is grey

More than five minutes have passed since measurement. The last value is retained but is not shown as recent. Its age increases even if a request returns the same reading. Check your sensor app, LibreLinkUp and Internet. Windows notices do not replace your main system's alarms.

The clock needs attention

Use Configurar / reparar el reloj or Ajustes → Visualización → Configurar el reloj de Windows. Follow the wizard's diagnosis. If Windhawk mods are paused, resume them in Windhawk. When El reloj está listo appears, choose Listo.

Clock integration is supported on Windows 11 x64 with the validated combination. Other Windhawk versions are not automatically downgraded. You can use the tray or large number instead. Compare views.

The graph is empty when I open the app

It uses readings received during the current session and needs at least two to draw a trend. Session history is lost when the app exits; it is not a full sensor history download.

I cannot find the icon or close the app

Open the Windows tray overflow arrow. Closing the panel keeps GlucoTick running. To exit, right-click its icon and choose Salir.

Installing and updating

Download the official latest Setup. GlucoTick checks for updates every two hours with up to five minutes of variation, and when starting or opening its views. You decide when to install. Save pending settings before updating; account and preferences are preserved.

Current installers have no trusted publisher signature. SHA-256 verifies integrity but is not a publisher signature. Use official downloads and do not disable Windows protections globally.

Uninstall and preserve your data

Uninstall GlucoTick through Windows Installed apps. Choose to keep or delete local account and settings. Its clock integration is disabled; Windhawk is kept.

Report a problem

Open an issue with app version, Windows version, affected view and reproduction steps. Acerca de can copy a diagnostic without account or glucose data. Review any logs before sharing them.

Reviewed on 8 October 2026 for GlucoTick 0.8.10. The Windows app is currently in Spanish; menu labels match the app.

Independent companion viewer

GlucoTick is not an official Abbott app. It does not calculate insulin doses or replace your primary FreeStyle Libre device, app, alarms or clinical advice. Shared readings may arrive late. Use your primary system for treatment decisions.
